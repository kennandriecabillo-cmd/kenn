The Problem

Soil is degrading faster than farmers can restore it

Two crises are converging on the same fields: worn-out soil, and water that doesn't stay long enough to matter.

60%

of land degradation happens on agricultural land

A third of all soils are already degraded, cutting yields for an estimated 1.7 billion people.

70%+

of soils show reduced productivity from degradation

Agriculture also consumes enormous volumes of water — most of it lost before it ever reaches the root zone.

↓

Significant fertilizer losses through leaching

Nutrients applied at cost wash away with the first heavy rain, taken with the water the soil couldn't hold.

🌾

Smallholders are hit hardest

Farmers with the least buffer absorb most of the yield loss from degraded, thirsty soil.

Source: Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations (FAO), 2025

What's actually in the bag

Every pellet is engineered as a dual-layer system: an outer nutrient-locking shell built on high-CEC biochar, wrapped around a water-retentive core.

Lab testing shows the pellet absorbs roughly 4–5× its own weight in water — releasing it slowly back into the root zone between irrigations or rainfall.

Traditional Methods

  • Require large volumes of compost or manure
  • Bulky, labor-intensive handling and application
  • Nutrients leach quickly after rainfall or irrigation

The EcoGro Way

  • Pelletized for easy transport, storage, and application
